MiniCPM5-1B, entirely in your browser

This demo downloads the model once (cached by your browser) and runs it locally with wllama (llama.cpp compiled to WebAssembly). Nothing you type leaves your machine.

  • 1B params · LlamaForCausalLM · Apache-2.0 (OpenBMB)
  • Think / No-Think hybrid reasoning modes from one checkpoint
  • Works best in Chrome/Edge with SIMD + threads; falls back to single-thread elsewhere
